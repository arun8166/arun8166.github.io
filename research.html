Joint Flow & Diffusion-based Framework for DoA Estimation

WiSDOM Lab, IIT Kanpur – Aug 2025 to present

A joint MeanFlow–DDIM model (6.86M parameters) that estimates the directions of arrival of multiple targets from a single noisy, cluttered snapshot received by an antenna array with impairments.

  • A conditional MeanFlow model generates multiple clean signal snapshots from each noisy observation.
  • A complex-valued Transformer encoder, using complex self-attention and antenna-correlation features, learns the spatial structure.
  • A Diffusion Transformer conditioned on both noisy and clean-signal embeddings draws posterior samples of the target angles.
  • Lower RMSE than conventional subspace methods and published CNN and diffusion-based baselines across SNR levels.
